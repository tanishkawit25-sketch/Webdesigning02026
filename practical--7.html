College Department FAQ

1. What are the college working hours?
College working hours are from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
2. How can I apply for leave?
Students can apply for leave through the college portal.
3. Where can I find the exam timetable?
The exam timetable is available on the department notice board and college website.
4. How can I contact the department?
You can contact the department office during working hours.
